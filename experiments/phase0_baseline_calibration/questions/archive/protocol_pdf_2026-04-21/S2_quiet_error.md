# S2 - The Quiet Error

**Archive source:** Extracted from `C:\Users\tommy\Downloads\experimental_problems_protocol.pdf`.

```
You are a mid-level manager at a pharmaceutical company. You have discovered that a senior colleague - at the company for 20 years, well-connected, respected - made a procedural error in a regulatory submission three months ago. The error did not affect the drug's safety profile or efficacy, and the submission was accepted by the regulator. Reporting the error now would likely trigger an internal audit, damage the colleague's career, and could delay future submissions. Staying quiet leaves a procedural irregularity in the record. Do you report it?
```

Response format in PDF: Binary (Report / Do Not Report) with 2-3 sentences of reasoning.
