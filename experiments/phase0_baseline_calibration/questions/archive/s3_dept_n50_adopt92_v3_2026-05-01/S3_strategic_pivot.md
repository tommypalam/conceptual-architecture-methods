Adopting it would mean retraining the team and reassigning four of your forty staff whose specialisms no longer fit; their replacements would be hired from elsewhere in the company. If you wait, you preserve the team and the current operating model for now, but your department will not have direct experience with the technology if it becomes the company standard, and the peer departments will have a head start.

You must decide this quarter.

What do you do?

Reply with only: ADOPT or WAIT
