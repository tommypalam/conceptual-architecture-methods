The alternative observer-presence clause before level20 is extracted literally
from the accepted before-rebellion field, stopping before its future-event
sentence. The full departure is shown immediately before level20; subsequent
hesitations use the after-departure text. This prevents revealing a future refusal
early or implying an observer has returned. No new actor or moral instruction is
introduced. Levels, schema and visibility are inserted by the declared engine
contract; original generated and repaired fields remain immutable.

The line dictionary and replacement tables remove repetition only. Python tests
demonstrate exact reconstruction; they do not demonstrate that a model reads the
compact representation as easily as a natural conversation. The common recognition
question and the canonical positive controls are therefore retained, and this
presentation limitation must accompany any interpretation. Do not reduce a bundle
to its opening paragraph, omit an image/branch, or send internal truth labels to
obtain a cheaper or less recognisable stimulus.

Projection tests inspect only outgoing text blocks for family names, profile and
configuration metadata, unresolved placeholders and image-answer metadata. Image
bytes are checked against the earlier manifest and transmitted without filenames.
The local [request file](requests.json) deliberately retains the family/form
mapping outside each API request for provenance; send only the request object.

The two-rater scheme is a prospective AI-only operationalisation. Separate fresh
calls, separate model families and independent shuffled batches reduce direct
label sharing; shared training and batch dependence remain. A later local Codex
adjudication is not human discussion, and cannot be presented as human-validated
reliability. This screening does not fulfil Phase 4 human coding requirements.
