## Design audit and question

Implementation specification sections7.2–7.3 require causal-structure preservation
and count an unambiguous defining structure as recognition. The successful
structural reviews and50/50 recognition in every alternative reveal a tension
between these requirements. This is not proof that every possible alternative
must fail, nor proof of training-data contamination.

The ultimatum game is a diagnostic first case because its one-proposer allocation
and veto-to-zero payoff rule are its defining structure. Before funding another
five-family regeneration/full screen, test ONE new surface setting. This does
not remove the other four benchmarks from scope. For Milgram, Asch, bystander and
reactance, the corresponding risk is that escalation under authority, perceptual
group pressure, diffusion of responsibility and restriction-driven preference
change also supply recognizable defining features. A new surface alone may not
solve that problem. Do not remove these mechanisms to obtain a lower recognition rate.
