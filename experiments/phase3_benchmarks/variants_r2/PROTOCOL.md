## Generation and independent reading

The generator receives domain, procedural invariants and an exact list of wording
fields. It does not receive benchmark names, context directions, profiles, human
rates or target decisions. Engine-controlled schedules/actions/scoring are fixed;
the generator supplies literal wording fragments and records unresolved changes
to the mechanism. The original unexecuted generation draft is superseded by this
complete structural packet, not overwritten or presented as a previous attempt.

The independent reader is Claude, different from the generator (GPT-5.4) and
planned executor (GPT-5.4-mini). Each fresh review receives the actual canonical
setup/event examples, full invariants and candidate wording. It returns accept,
revise or reject plus concrete mismatches and limits. Accept cannot coexist with
blocking mismatches or required changes. This is an independent LLM reading, not
human expert approval. No live human stimuli or personal data are sent.

The documents describe AI procedural analogues. Neither a source-matched human
rate nor literal equivalence of pain, financial motivation, social interaction
and computer warnings is claimed. Do not require those lived experiences to
exist in an LLM; do flag changed agency, causal incentives, urgency or information.
The accepted neutral context and canonical parameter distributions cannot be
redesigned by this reader.

Local review checks every finding against the exact package; an LLM's accepted
status is not automatically a release. Passing text review does not establish
image-difficulty equivalence. Rejected/revision-needed candidates do not receive
paid recognition probes yet. No regenerate-until-pass loop: revisions get their
own designation and review after the round's findings are assessed.
