## What this first stage can establish

Develop all five canonical procedures and their alternative-domain candidates.
Test whether the proposed alternatives preserve the required causal structure
before paying for population outcomes. A rejection is useful design evidence;
neither passing this review nor passing recognition establishes human resemblance
or ethical understanding. There are **zero sampled agents** in this stage.

1. Record source corrections and explicit measurement units prospectively.
2. Generate one candidate per structural packet with a model different from the
   intended benchmark executor (GPT-5.4 mini). Save exact requests and responses.
3. Have an independent reader evaluate the structure against both procedures.
   An LLM review is labelled as such; it is not human sign-off. Unresolved
   load-bearing differences block that candidate. No automatic rewrite loop.
4. After structural acceptance, freeze the complete stimulus bundle for
   recognition. Use Claude/Gemini in fresh conversations with no profile,
   normative context, benchmark name, source citation or crosswalk. N=50 per
   canonical/alternative bundle, 500 calls if all five pairs are eligible.
5. Two independent raters classify recognition; retain disagreements and
   adjudication. More than 15 recognised outputs out of 50 rejects an alternative.
   Missing or invalid outputs block a complete N=50 decision. Recognition of
   canonical versions is reported as a positive control, not grounds for removal.

Only one candidate generation per benchmark is planned in round 1. Revisions
get a new designation and linked records; do not keep generating until a pass.
The separate recognition release must fix the exact judge, prompts, response
limit, rater identities and all-call cost bound before dispatch. No candidate
may advance on the basis of its generator's self-assessment.

## Five-perspective review

Decision: release a small scenario-development stage under the newly authorised
$30 evening ceiling, retaining all five benchmark families.

Linden: preserve the distinction between response generation, human comparison
and moral evaluation. Osei: assess whether stakes, uncertainty and timing survive
domain changes; do not certify variants because they sound plausible. Tanaka:
recognition samples are repeated model outputs, not 50 independent human judges;
report that limitation and preserve the fixed denominator. Renna: keep the ten
agent parameters intact downstream; no behavioural profiles are needed for this
stage. Okafor: stop after one development round and spend only after accounting
checks. Disagreement: convenient surface changes can alter the mechanism itself.

recognition or population collection. These are synthetic perspectives, not the
