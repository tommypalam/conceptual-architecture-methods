# Operational procedures for independent review

Drafts, not released population tasks. All stimuli must be inspectable before
their hashes are frozen. The human reference, simulated procedure and measured
outcome are separate fields. No human response rate is included in an agent prompt.

## Authority: escalation with a stopping decision

Use a sequence of decisions with an explicit current intervention level, observed
recipient response, and experimenter request. The 1963 remote-feedback procedure
starts at 15 volts with 15-volt increments to 450; pounding occurs at 300 and
315, followed by silence. Do not mix this with later vocal-feedback conditions.
On reluctance, there are up to four successive authority prompts before a final
refusal ends the trajectory. An irrevocable quit remains possible. Preserve the
whole history and reinject the profile/context at each decision. Report maximum
level and completion; never fill unvisited steps with imputed choices.

The exact error/correct-answer schedule, initial demonstration, assurance wording,
escape actions and modulator scripts need source extraction and review before
execution. The candidate generator is given these as unresolved constraints,
not permission to invent a claim of exact replication. Corporate feedback may
preserve escalation and authority yet lose perceived physical danger: this is
a substantive equivalence question that can reject the spec's example.

[Original procedure](https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Influence_Compliance/Milgrim_1963_Behavioral_study_of_obedience.pdf).

## Loyalty: repeated perceptual judgments

Render reference and comparison stimuli without labelling the correct alternative
in text. Canonical line lengths and alternative product colours each need
unambiguous ground truth plus isolated no-peer competence checks. Correct positions
and majority-wrong positions must be counterbalanced. A trial presents peers'
answers before the participant's answer. Critical trials contain an incorrect
majority; neutral trials retain correct peer judgments. Keep previous participant
answers and the public/private distinction explicit.

Primary response is each participant's fraction of critical trials agreeing with
the wrong majority; inference clusters trials by participant. Report no-peer
accuracy separately. Freeze the trial count and images after checking the source
and model's perceptual competence, before population outcomes. Line and colour
difficulty cannot be assumed equal. Images incur separately reserved input cost.

## Justice: two roles in an allocation game

Proposer and responder act in isolated conversations. A fixed 100-unit stake is
divided in integer units; acceptance implements the split and rejection gives
both zero. State anonymity, one-shot interaction and no previous entitlement in
both domains. Record proposer offer/100. A responder receives a specific offer;
the 20-unit offer measures conditional rejection. Additional offered shares need
separate, predeclared slots; do not derive a whole threshold from one binary choice.

The candidate domain uses a discretionary bonus, but a shared employer, earned
entitlements or future dealings may change incentives. Those differences are
explicit review criteria. Simulated units do not have the motivational status of
real money paid to human participants. The spec's Justice-low/high predicted
rejection direction is retained as a proposed hypothesis, not inferred by changing
parameter meanings or relabelling after responses.

## Care: helping opportunity and other potential helpers

Select one canonical emergency procedure before collection. The present structural
packet uses an audible distress event with either no other potential helper or
three others able to hear it. Participants cannot see whether others have acted.
Keep urgency, ability to contact assistance, cost and the observation window equal
across the presence contrast. Record individual intervention; whether anyone in
a group intervenes is a different outcome. Simulated steps are not human seconds.

A software-incident alternative must state who is at risk and what help changes.
Replacing distress with an inconsequential technical warning can remove the care
mechanism. Danger and intervention cost must be separate variables: a generic
danger flag does not reproduce every meta-analytic moderator. Exact temporal
procedure and human comparison remain pending; no universal 75%/55% pass band.

## Freedom: restriction and preference restoration

First elicit a ranking of a fixed set of alternatives in a separate stage. Then
apply a choice restriction or a matched control intervention, followed by a new
ranking and choice. Freeze the rule selecting the removed option and the definition
of adjacency before data. The counterpart in control must be designated by the
same rule even when it remains available. Do not count a logically impossible
choice as evidence of low reactance.

Keep preference change, choice of an available substitute and protest distinct.
The specification's adjacent-option binary endpoint needs an explicit task and
source match; it is not yet a validated substitute for preference restoration.
No silent switch to anger scores or a new endpoint. The generator must flag this
gap and can supply a draft scene, not invent a historical probability target.

## Shared release requirements

Freeze contexts using the five original concepts, with off-target NEUTRAL distinct
from LOW. Review axis text against canonical definitions; do not invent a mapping
from individual parameter values to preferred responses. Keep the same profile
across predeclared context comparisons and isolate conversations across conditions.
Choose and freeze analysis, missingness handling, sample sizes and role/trial
costs before collecting behavioural outcomes. These procedural details are being
resolved under the researcher's approved reconciliation, not claimed already tested.
