# Prospective Phase 3 source and inference erratum

Accepted reconciliation: 2026-09-13, before new Phase 3 behavioural responses.
This note supplements the historical thesis/specification; it does not rewrite
their bytes or retroactively change a completed experiment.

1. Rains (2013) reports **20 studies and 4,942 participants**, not 123 studies.
   Its reactance model combines anger and counterarguments. A standardized effect
   is not automatically a 20-percentage-point adjacent-choice difference.
   [Publication](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-2958.2012.01443.x).
2. The Fischer et al. bystander synthesis supports a context-dependent aggregate
   effect; it does not establish the proposed universal 75%/55% software-incident
   rates. Do not mix smoke and seizure procedures or individual and group units.
   [Publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21534650/).
3. Ultimatum overall rejection is not rejection conditional on a 20% offer.
   Proposer shares and responder decisions require separate denominators.
   [Author manuscript](https://econwpa.ub.uni-muenchen.de/econ-wp/exp/papers/0401/0401003.pdf).
4. Milgram's final-voltage outcome concerns an escalation trajectory; Asch's
   conformity outcome concerns critical perceptual trials. Neither can be
   replaced by a single hypothetical question and retain identical measurement.
5. The original high/low contrast thresholds remain recorded as theory-derived
   targets pending source-matched operationalisation. They are not newly verified
   human estimates. Canonical rates and modulator rates cannot automatically
   supply expected differences between political-ethical context blocks.
6. Scenario recognition and modulator failure are different diagnostics. Neither
   an unrecognised scenario nor a correct modulator pattern proves an absence of
   training-data influence. A failed pattern may reflect model or measurement
   failure and is not automatically classified as contamination.
