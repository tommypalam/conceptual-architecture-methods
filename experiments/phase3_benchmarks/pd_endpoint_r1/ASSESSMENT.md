## Findings and disposition

   is explicitly framed as violating the published procedure."* **Accepted.**
   Option wording ("without the sign-off the procedure requires") read as
   lawful-versus-unlawful rather than two permissible choices. r2 states that the
   procedure is internal guidance the role may set aside at discretion, that
   neither option breaches a binding rule and neither carries a sanction, and
   rewords options as "setting aside ... in this case".
2. *"The design text itself refers to 'task text is identical across study arms'
   and 'manipulation'."* **Accepted; a drafting error.** Meta-description of the
   experiment was placed in the review packet. r2's packet contains task content
   only, and its gate refuses to build a batch if the packet carries any non-task
   key or any experiment-describing term. Verified against this exact mistake.
3. *"Some task texts contain cues toward a preferred option via
   deadline/availability facts."* **Not accepted.** Those facts constitute the
   dilemma; removing them leaves no decision. Recorded as a stated limit.
