## Question and fidelity contract

The prior study observed a 50-point profile-package difference at offer 20 in
both justice contexts, but zero primary context interaction. Definitions,
numeric positions, instruction wording and length were bundled in that E/U
comparison. This follow-up asks whether the existing structured decimal profile
and an exact-information prose rendering produce different decisions.

E is the previous canonical encoded system prompt, unchanged. V replaces only
the profile block with ten ordinary sentences. Each retains the exact trait
name and both endpoint descriptions from E, and writes the same rendered
position in words. Example: 0.63 becomes "sixty-three percent of the way from
[zero-endpoint description] toward [one-endpoint description]". Endpoint text
is quoted literally apart from whitespace normalization. Trait order and
direction remain unchanged, particularly LL external-to-internal.

V removes Arabic-number notation and list formatting from the profile block;
it does NOT remove numerical information. Its written percentages are fully
recoverable to E's two-decimal precision. It does not replace coordinates with
unvalidated categories such as low/moderate/high, probabilities of actions,
behavioural mixtures or new trait-to-action rules. Percent denotes location on
the same conceptual continuum, not a validated behavioural probability.
The V introduction explicitly states that distinction. The experiment tests
presentation/notation with matched information, not architecture versus all
semantic instruction, and not numeric versus wholly nonquantitative psychology.

An offline inverse parser must recover all ten rendered values, names and
endpoints exactly for all 24 profiles in both contexts. Tests cover every
integer percentage from zero through one hundred, endpoint directions, unchanged
nonprofile text, unchanged tasks, saved-file reconstruction and shared block
assignment. Independent Claude review receives the complete contract/protocol,
both representations in both contexts for profile 1, all six tasks, metadata
and the exhaustive-fidelity summary. Review acceptance does not establish
psychometric equivalence or human validity. No generated free paraphrase is used.

## Analysis and interpretation

The primary exploratory endpoint remains rejection at offer 20. Freeze three
contrasts: E-minus-V at Justice LOW, E-minus-V at Justice HIGH, and the difference
between those representation effects (LOW minus HIGH). No preferred sign.
All three are descriptive; no significance, equivalence, pass threshold or
post-results confirmation claim. Context LOW/HIGH denotes institutions, not
individual traits or moral quality. Secondary: E and V context contrasts,
all five rejection rates, mean proposer offers and complete-grid monotonicity.

Reuse the prior paired-block bootstrap (9999 resamples) and conservative bounded
Hoeffding intervals, analysis seeds 2026091322 through 2026091326. Simple
differences are bounded [-1,1], the interaction [-2,2]. Report all assigned
profiles, missingness identification bounds, complete-block supplements and
degenerate bootstrap flags. Missing outcomes block the complete-data estimate;
do not silently filter profiles. No tests, adjusted p-values or human-rate bands.
Independent/stationary-block assumptions limit all intervals.

Similar estimates with wide intervals do not demonstrate equivalence. A
difference establishes sensitivity to this presentation change, not a benefit,
moral superiority or intrinsic understanding. Matching values and endpoint
words does not match token length, layout, unit framing (decimals versus
percentages) or processing difficulty; these remain bundled format differences.
No result identifies ten separate trait mechanisms or solves contamination.
More rejection is not inherently good or bad.

## Five-perspective design review

Decision: compare two information-matched presentations without arbitrary bins.
These are synthetic perspectives, not external expert review or extra API calls.

- Linden: written numbers are still quantitative; do not call V nonnumeric or
  treat presentation sensitivity as ethical understanding.
- Osei: preserving endpoint wording helps fidelity; ordinary sentences with
  explicit percentages are narrower than a naturalistic personality description.
- Tanaka: use new matched profiles, retain all cells and wide uncertainty;
  neither a zero difference nor a nonsignificant result demonstrates equivalence.
- Renna: this step tests whether the numerical table format adds something;
  successful transfer would support format robustness, not eliminate semantic prompting.
- Okafor: deterministic forward/inverse rendering, exact saved requests,
  independent review and inherited-ledger checks avoid paid translation retries.

The tension is naturalness versus exact value preservation. Resolution: preserve
information and explicitly narrow the claim to notation/presentation. A later
fully nonquantitative comparator would require its own validated translation,
not a retrospective relabelling of this arm.
