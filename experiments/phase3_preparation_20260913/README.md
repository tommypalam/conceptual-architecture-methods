## Scope to prepare

Follow Part 7 of Theory/implementation_specification_v0_1.md:

- All five benchmarks: Milgram (authority), Asch (loyalty), Ultimatum Game
  (justice), Bystander (care), and Reactance (freedom).
- Canonical and decanonised forms, with independent review of causal-structure
  equivalence before use (sections 7.1-7.2).
- Separate-model recognition probes, two-rater classification, and the specified
  recognition threshold and revision procedure (section 7.3).
- Predicted-high versus predicted-low contexts for each benchmark, with matched
  profiles and frozen hypotheses (section 7.4).
- Milgram and Asch modulator conditions (section 7.5).
- R-matrix and copula sensitivity analyses (section 7.6).

These targeted contexts can broaden concept coverage beyond the two Phase 2
anchors. They are not automatically an exhaustive or balanced sample of the 32
binary societies. Phase 3 benchmark results cannot substitute for missing S1-S3
or C1-C3 results in those societies. An eventual Phase 2 extension needs a new
prospective study designation, sampling plan and analysis; preserve existing
records and keep any reuse or pooling explicit.

## Items to resolve before freezing collection

1. Build a clause-by-clause spec checklist and inventory existing prompt/runner
   support. Specify the behavioural outcome for each benchmark, human reference
   evidence and limits of comparability. Do not turn scenario instructions into
   instructions to reproduce a known percentage.
2. Freeze exact high/low context texts. Section 7.1 uses 'others neutral' for
   several benchmarks; a neutral dimension is not a third value already present
   in the binary Phase 2 grid. Resolve its representation explicitly instead of
   silently substituting one of the previous whole-context anchors.
3. Define paired sampling, counterpart baselines, primary contrasts, uncertainty,
   multiplicity, stopping rules and handling of invalid responses prospectively.
   Resolve any differences between prose and pseudocode. Do not describe a
   recognition probe or a rate match as proof of internal ethical understanding.
4. Prepare structural specifications and the independent variant-review workflow,
   then the distinct-model recognition workflow, before releasing variants.
   Human reference comparisons remain separate from Phase 4 moral coding.
5. Expand the budget into an exact schedule. Section 7.7 estimates about 11,000
   calls, but sections 7.5-7.6 contain approximate modulator counts, 100 weak-R
   perturbations and three t-copula degrees of freedom. Resolve replication and
   overlap explicitly; the headline estimate is not an executable cost ceiling.
   Preserve the user's minimize-spend preference and hard $100 limit; no new
   spending authority or budget reset is inferred from moving phases.
6. Prepare and check the recording, parsing and replay harness offline, then
   freeze the reviewed protocol, prompts, model versions, sample and budget.
   Any necessary departure from the spec must be explicit before collection.
