# Reactance: structural review packet

Status: draft. No validated canonical or decanonised prompt is released.

## Behaviour to measure

Threat-versus-control change in preference or adjacent-option selection; define adjacency and response denominator before conversion to a binary rate.

## Actors

- participant choosing options
- authority restricting a choice

## Required structure

- Credible initial freedom to choose
- An explicit removal/threat versus a comparable no-threat condition
- Specified preference measurement before/after where required
- Measure restoration separately from simple inability to select the removed option

## Candidate settings from the spec

Canonical: Choice alternatives with a social restriction; exact stimulus set and intervention are unresolved.

Decanonised: Project-priority resource restriction (spec example).

## Independent review questions

- Ranking shifts, anger and binary selection are different outcomes
- A standardized mean difference cannot specify a unique probability difference without distribution and base-rate assumptions
- Removal changes feasible options; compare against a carefully matched control

Preserve agency, information, incentives, pressure, available actions, timing and
outcome measurement. Reject a variant that changes a load-bearing element.
Do not include target human percentages in prompts shown to simulated agents.
