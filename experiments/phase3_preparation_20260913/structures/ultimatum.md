# Ultimatum: structural review packet

Status: draft. No validated canonical or decanonised prompt is released.

## Behaviour to measure

Keep proposer offer share separate from responder acceptance at each offered share; do not infer a rejection threshold from one offer.

## Actors

- proposer
- responder

## Required structure

- Fixed divisible stake
- Proposer selects split
- Responder acceptance implements split; rejection pays both zero
- Role decisions isolated to avoid contamination from answering the other role

## Candidate settings from the spec

Canonical: Anonymous monetary ultimatum bargain.

Decanonised: Discretionary team-bonus negotiation (spec example).

## Independent review questions

- Workplace entitlement or recurring relationships may change the payoff game
- Hypothetical points lack real monetary incentives
- Primary 20-percent offer alone cannot test proposer distribution or equal-offer acceptance

Preserve agency, information, incentives, pressure, available actions, timing and
outcome measurement. Reject a variant that changes a load-bearing element.
Do not include target human percentages in prompts shown to simulated agents.
