# Bystander: structural review packet

Status: draft. No validated canonical or decanonised prompt is released.

## Behaviour to measure

Intervention within a defined observation window, distinguishing individual helping from whether any member of a group helps.

## Actors

- participant observing emergency
- person at risk or incident source
- other potential helpers

## Required structure

- Same observable emergency and same opportunity to help
- Manipulate whether other helpers are present without implying that they already acted
- Keep danger, intervention cost, knowledge and time window fixed in a presence contrast

## Candidate settings from the spec

Canonical: A staged emergency observed alone or alongside other potential helpers; exact paradigm remains to be selected.

Decanonised: Workplace software incident (spec example).

## Independent review questions

- Smoke/pluralistic ignorance and seizure/diffusion of responsibility are different procedures
- Risk to people versus software reliability changes stakes
- A model statement of intent is not timed human intervention

Preserve agency, information, incentives, pressure, available actions, timing and
outcome measurement. Reject a variant that changes a load-bearing element.
Do not include target human percentages in prompts shown to simulated agents.
