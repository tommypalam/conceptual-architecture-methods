# Milgram: structural review packet

Status: draft. No validated canonical or decanonised prompt is released.

## Behaviour to measure

Completion of the maximum step in an escalating compliance trajectory; not merely agreeing to one step.

## Actors

- participant acting as teacher
- experimenter with procedural authority
- learner believed to receive shocks

## Required structure

- Increasing aversive intervention after errors
- Learner protest and pressure to continue
- Participant may refuse; preserve the escalation and stop rule

## Candidate settings from the spec

Canonical: Teacher/learner learning experiment; increasing shock labels and scripted experimenter prompts.

Decanonised: Corporate evaluator delivering escalating critical feedback under supervisor pressure (spec example).

## Independent review questions

- Physical pain versus reputational discomfort changes stakes; not automatically equivalent
- Cannot tell participant that harm is fake or that learner is a confederate
- Single final-step vignette does not reproduce a maximum-voltage trajectory

Preserve agency, information, incentives, pressure, available actions, timing and
outcome measurement. Reject a variant that changes a load-bearing element.
Do not include target human percentages in prompts shown to simulated agents.
