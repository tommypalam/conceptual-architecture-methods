## Primary screen and uncertainty

Compare all six wording pairs within each of the four profiles:24 contrasts.
Compute16 exact binomial cell intervals, each with tail probability.05/(2*16).
Together they have at least95% simultaneous coverage by Bonferroni. Subtract
bounds to obtain simultaneous intervals for all24 rate differences; include
missing or invalid outcomes as unknown rather than silently dropping them.

If any contrast's interval is entirely outside[-.10,+.10], detect a gross local
wording failure. Report every contrast and count, not only the worst or best.
If none is detected, the result is unresolved, NOT equivalence or a phase pass.
The.10 margin comes from the existing robustness objective; this diagnostic
screen is not a revised implementation of the original equivalence gate.

N30 with simultaneous bounds is designed to screen for large failures, not
establish small differences or the full equivalence claim. Near-equal observed
rates, saturation or no detected failure cannot validate this selected setup.
No increasingN afterward to chase a favourable result.

Secondary, nominal across comparisons: MoR endpoint effects at both PD levels
within each wording and high-PD minus low-PD endpoint-effect contrasts, with
conservative exact intervals. Two endpoints do not assess monotonicity, smooth
curves or reproduce the previous slope interaction. Parameter-to-decision
meaning and internal reasoning are not newly coded here.

## Interpretation and limits

A detected difference limits transport of the local fixed-template result to
reviewed alternative descriptions. It does not prove that ethical encoding is
impossible. No detected difference leaves uncertainty; it does not erase the
original8/30 equivalence result. Selected PD/MoR corners on known S3 cannot
validate all parameters or problems. All ten endpoint descriptions vary together,
so a wording effect cannot be assigned to PD or MoR wording alone.

Original theory, all five concepts, ten parameters and Phase0 locks remain.
No Phase2 launch, reduced architecture, full battery or new intervention is
automatically commissioned by the result. The900-call confirmation stays paused.
