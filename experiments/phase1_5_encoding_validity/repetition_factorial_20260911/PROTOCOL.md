## Questions and fixed inference

Primary: the verbal-only average ADOPT rate, equally weighting .1/.9, under
repetition minus original. Use a conservative >=95% interval from four exact
binomial cell bounds. An interval entirely below zero supports lower ADOPT
rates with repetition for these tested conditions. It does not by itself show
that endpoint sensitivity is lower: a baseline shift can preserve the gap.

Secondary: each of the six within-text/representation .9-minus-.1 effects,
and three direct repetition-minus-original differences in those effects.
Use exact conservative intervals with missing/invalid observations treated
as unknown, following the existing contrast implementation. Secondary intervals
are nominal, not adjusted across the secondary family or prior exploration.
No inference of an arm difference merely from different significance labels.

This is an endpoint diagnostic. It neither applies the five-value monotonicity
gate nor closes it. It cannot establish retained slopes, paraphrase equivalence,
joint encoding, faithful reasoning, internal understanding or AGI. Known S3
and the candidate were selected during development; do not call this a general
holdout. Interpretation assumes independent responses under the frozen setup.

All 720 allocations must be recorded. At N60, >=98% valid permits at most one
invalid parse per scientific cell; unknown-outcome envelopes remain in the
reported intervals. Any API/model/usage failure invalidates the screen and
stops collection after the active batch. Preserve failures and pre-call intents;
no automatic retry, replacement, outcome-based stopping, or additional calls.

## Decisions after collection

- Negative primary interval: stop treating repetition as a repair of the verbal
  floor; original remains the reference. Read the sensitivity contrasts too.
- Primary interval includes zero: causal suppression is unresolved. Do not call
  the texts equivalent or repetition validated, and do not automatically grow N.
- Positive primary interval: report the unexpected direction and retain the
  same limits; higher ADOPT probability alone is not better ethical encoding.
- Positive original endpoint effects can motivate a separately frozen full-curve
  confirmation of an eligible candidate. Endpoint success cannot replace the
  original battery or justify confirmation of all ten parameters by extrapolation.
