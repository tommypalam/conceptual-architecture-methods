## Primary result: retention is not assessable

The fresh canonical repetition curve has ADOPT counts **0/8/4/11/8 of 30**
at MoR .1/.3/.5/.7/.9. Its fitted slope is positive, 2.036 (95% Wald interval
[.523,3.550], LR p=.00606), but observed rates decline at two steps. It therefore
fails the frozen monotonicity requirement. This is not evidence of zero
parameter response, nor proof that the underlying response function declines.
The fresh control is ineligible under the agreed rule, so both representation
retention assessments are **not assessable**, not completed failed retention
tests and not passes. The earlier favourable canonical curve is not substituted.

Numeric-only has a fitted slope of -.558 (95% interval [-2.458,1.341],
LR p=.563). This does not establish a positive gradient or a genuine reversed
effect. Verbal-only is almost fixed at WAIT: 149 of 150 responses. Its nominal
slope is 2.851 with a very wide interval [-5.547,11.249], driven by only one
ADOPT response. The displayed verbal/canonical point ratio of 1.40 cannot be
interpreted as successful retention: the control is ineligible and the verbal
fit has extremely sparse decision variation. Original thresholds are unchanged.

The previous canonical repetition curve was 0/1/1/5/12 and met the directional
criterion; this fresh one does not. These are separate batches and are not
pooled or selected for a favourable verdict. We have not established a
between-batch distribution change merely from different pass/fail outcomes.
We also have not demonstrated an improvement over the original non-repeated
implementation, which was not collected as a concurrent control here.

## Decision and next work

Do not scale repetition into a full battery on these results. Added repetition
has not demonstrated a reliable repair across the tested representations.
The original canonical MoR response remains evidence of local parameter
influence; neither these screens nor their failures settle the possibility
of encoding ethics in the full architecture.

Before another paid intervention, use the saved data to separate two issues:
the finite-sample behaviour of the observed-monotonicity rule, and the loss
of decision variation in the representation variants. An offline precision
analysis can quantify the first under explicitly stated model assumptions;
it cannot waive the rule or explain away the second. Inspect existing variant
rationales against unchanged meanings to locate specific interpretation gaps.
Do not keep adding overlay text or rerunning small samples until one passes.

Any subsequent candidate should address a documented gap, retain all ten
parameters and five concepts, and have its exact prompts and spending cap
frozen before dispatch. A theoretical departure still requires a demonstrated
physical/technical limitation and consultation with the researcher.
No Phase 0 text, theory, scoring threshold or frozen collection code changed.
The optional tool remains inactive, Phase 1.5 is open, and Phase 2 stays on hold.
