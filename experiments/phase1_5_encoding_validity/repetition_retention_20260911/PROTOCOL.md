## Primary diagnostic and interpretation

Use the original directional rule on the **fresh canonical repetition curve**:
complete allocation, >=98% valid in each cell, positive logistic slope with
LR p<.05, endpoint Cohen's h>=.20, and monotonic observed rates. At N30 the
quality rule requires all 30 responses in each cell to be valid.

Only if that control meets its criterion are the two retention checks
assessable. Each needs complete, >=98%-valid coverage, an estimable positive
slope and absolute variant/canonical fitted slope ratio >=.50. These are the
unchanged original retention requirements: variant monotonicity and variant
slope p<.05 are not extra requirements. Report them descriptively, with fitted
slopes and their intervals. The ratio threshold is a point-estimate rule, not
a confidence bound or statistical equivalence test.

Primary diagnostic outcome: both checks met, at least one not met, or not
assessable because the fresh canonical criterion is unmet. A failed, pinned
or separated control cannot be rescued by substituting the previous curve.
Missing/invalid collection receives incomplete/invalid status, not a pass.
No historical responses are pooled. The model's responses are independent
calls conditional on the frozen setup; original p-values are not adjusted
across the wider exploratory programme.

If the control or either retention requirement fails, do not launch a full
repetition battery automatically. If both checks are met, paraphrase robustness,
reasoning interpretation and independent confirmation still remain. No result
here establishes ethical understanding, moral correctness, AGI, or Phase 1.5
closure. All five concepts, ten parameters and current gates remain unchanged.
