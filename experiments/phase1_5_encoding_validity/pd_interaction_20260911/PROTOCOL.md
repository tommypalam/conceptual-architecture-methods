## Why this contrast

The previous mean-valued background is already the original full-ten control;
repeating it would not create a new control. PD is selected because thesis

## Primary and decision rules

Primary: fitted MoR logistic slope at PD=.9 minus slope at PD=.1. Independent
arm variance is the sum of slope variances; report Wald95%CI and two-sided p.
This assumes independent binomial calls and linear logits. Pinned or separated
fits yield non-estimability; no replacement primary selected after results.

CI excluding zero supports a scoped background-value effect on the fitted
MoR response under fixed descriptions and length. CI including zero is unresolved,
not equivalence and not proof of a presentation mechanism. Even a clear result
would not prove faithful psychological moderation, quantify prompt capacity,
or attribute the prior deletion effect entirely to PD.

Secondary: all ten observed rates with Wilson95%CI, direct difference in endpoint
probability changes with conservative exact bounds, and within-arm monotonicity.
Secondary intervals are nominal. Legacy directional summaries are descriptive
only in these modified backgrounds. Monotonicity is NOT a condition for the
primary interaction estimate; a miss remains a miss under the original phase
rule and cannot be waived or treated as proof of intrinsic nonmonotonicity.
No phase-pass, paraphrase-validity or reduced-architecture claim follows.

Stop on API/model/usage/reservation failure; save every attempt and intent,
no retry or in-place replacement. Any terminal failure invalidates the diagnostic;
otherwise require full allocation and>=98% valid per cell for its status.
No automatic follow-up or full sweep, regardless of the outcome.
