# Accepted normative encoding objective

Updated 12 September 2026. **Phase 1.5 is closed for this limited objective.**

> In the tested model, harness and dilemmas, explicit normative profiles can
> systematically influence generated decisions. Replicated Procedural Dependence
> effects provide an initial demonstration of functional normative parameterization.

The researcher accepted this limited conclusion after the results. This is a
disclosed scope decision, not a retrospective pass of the original battery or
a preregistered replacement threshold. The original full battery remains unmet.

Profiles enter before generation and the application retains raw model decisions
without ethical answer substitution. This supports an upstream behavioural
intervention. It does not identify latent model computations or provider-side
alignment, establish ethical understanding, or demonstrate persistent learning.
Independent PD confirmation and later canonical/verbal effects support the claim;
wording and representation limits, incomplete slots and all ten outcomes remain
in the evidence account. New profiles do not establish unseen-dilemma transfer.
