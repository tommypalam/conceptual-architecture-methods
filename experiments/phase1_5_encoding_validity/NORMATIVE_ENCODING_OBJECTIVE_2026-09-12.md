# Research objective: evidence that normative parameterisation is possible

Researcher clarification, 2026-09-12: the priority is a defensible conclusion that
encoding occurs to some extent and AI behaviour can be normatively parameterised.
The researcher rejected closing Phase 1.5 with the gate unmet. That earlier

## Target claim and present evidence

The target is: **explicitly represented normative dispositions can systematically
and predictably influence an AI agent's decisions under stated conditions**.
This is a behavioural claim about the implemented representation. It does not
require consciousness or establish internal ethical understanding, permanent
learning, moral improvement, or generalisation to every model or dilemma.

The current evidence is preliminary support, not merely a hope of a future result:

- MoR interventions have repeatedly influenced response choices, including an
  average +57.5 percentage-point ADOPT endpoint effect across 20 new backgrounds
  in the latest transfer study. MoR primarily concerns response style, so it is
  not independently a demonstration of normative content being encoded.
- MS more directly concerns normative orientation: the breadth of moral concern.
  Its +27.5 percentage-point FORMAL_REPORT endpoint effect across the same new
  backgrounds supports its predicted influence in S2. However, its 7.5/2.5/35%
  three-level rates are unordered, and earlier fixed-profile replications were
  unstable. The endpoint result alone does not establish reliable graded encoding.
- RE's predicted transfer effect remains unresolved. Preserve it alongside the
  positive findings rather than removing it from the evidentiary account.

See the [transfer result](profile_transfer_20260912/analysis/INTERPRETATION.md)
for multiplicity-adjusted intervals, all backgrounds and methodological limits.
No observations are reanalysed, pooled or discarded in this objective clarification.

Both S2 actions may have defensible normative reasons in the locked dilemma.
FORMAL_REPORT is the theory's directional hypothesis, not a newly imposed label
for morally better behaviour. The concept-to-parameter mapping remains a modelling
commitment; observing one predicted choice effect does not validate the full mapping
from five concepts to ten parameters or identify the model's internal mechanism.

## What would strengthen the inference

The next design should distinguish a repeatable parameter-linked normative
tendency from an isolated favourable endpoint, generic numerical salience or
an instruction to select a particular answer. Relevant evidence includes fresh
numeric interventions under fixed descriptions, predicted direction, independent
replication, intermediate-value behaviour and meaningful parameter specificity.
These are evidentiary objectives, not newly approved numerical gate thresholds.

Perfect invariance to all wording is not necessary for a conditional behavioural
claim. Nevertheless, observed wording and representation dependence constrain its
scope and cannot be omitted. The old failed criteria remain recorded. A narrower
claim cannot retroactively be presented as passing the original four-part battery.

The existing MoR/MS evidence justifies investigating conditional normative
parameterisation. It does not currently justify assuming that the full architecture
is validated, that general ethical understanding has been demonstrated, or that
the proposed larger numeric sweep would repair the outstanding original gates.
