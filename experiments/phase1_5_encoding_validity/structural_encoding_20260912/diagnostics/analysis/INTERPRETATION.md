# Representation diagnostics completed; no broad robustness pass established

480/480 valid responses; estimated token cost $0.51559875. All eight fresh
backgrounds, both selected parameters, five values and six representations are
retained. Exact requests, model/settings, distinct returned IDs, raw reparsing and
same-computer archive byte equality verified. The all-curve figure was inspected.

MoR's endpoint direction is positive in all six representations. Canonical rates
are 37.5/37.5/50/75/75%, an ordered observed curve. The primary adjusted bootstrap
interval for its canonical endpoint effect nevertheless includes zero. Only the
MoR third-paraphrase endpoint has a positive lower bound in the frozen family of
24 percentile-bootstrap intervals.

MS is less stable. Canonical rates are 37.5/12.5/25/12.5/50%; its intermediate
contrast is zero. All four wordings have positive observed endpoint differences,
but numeric-only reverses direction (37.5% to 12.5%) and verbal-only rises (37.5%
to 62.5%). The sample is too small to identify a general mechanism or establish
all-pair equivalence. No definition or representation is chosen for replacement.

## Important small-sample uncertainty qualification

An additional, explicitly **post-primary** exact paired-binomial sensitivity
check covers both contrasts across all twelve groups, with Holm adjustment over
24 tests. No comparison has adjusted p<.05. For the third-paraphrase MoR endpoint,
the eight backgrounds yield five positive discordant pairs, zero negative pairs
and three ties: the exact two-sided unadjusted p is .0625. This illustrates why
the positive lower bound of a small-sample percentile bootstrap should not alone
be treated as a well-calibrated discovery here.

The frozen outputs are unchanged. This sensitivity check was specified after
viewing the diagnostic outcomes and before interpreting the full sweep; it is not
an original preregistered test or a substitute gate. It will be applied uniformly
to the completed sweep's contrasts. Independent sampled backgrounds and stable
request handling are assumptions; provider/time dependence remains a limitation.
Exact non-detection is not evidence that the effects are zero.

## Implication for the structural objective

The evidence still motivates testing MoR's numerical control, and MS's dependence
on representation remains a serious limitation for normative encoding. These
diagnostics do not demonstrate intrinsic ethical understanding or repair the
original paraphrase/representation/recovery gates. The authorised all-parameter
canonical sweep proceeds according to its already-frozen coverage plan; it does
not inherit a pass from this panel. No interim favourable subset is selected.
