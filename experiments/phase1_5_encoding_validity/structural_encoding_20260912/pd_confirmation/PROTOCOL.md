# Independent PD confirmation following the all-ten sweep

Frozen before collection, 2026-09-12. This is a new study following inspection of
the complete 3,000-response sweep. PD/S2 and PD/S3 were exploratory in that sweep;
their directions are prospective hypotheses only for this new confirmation.
This is not a relabeling of the original nine theory-prespecified predictions.

## Question and primary decision

Does increasing Procedural Dependence, with other coordinates held fixed,
increase FORMAL_REPORT on S2 and WAIT on S3 in independently sampled backgrounds?
The conceptual interpretation is process-oriented decision generation: formal
review in S2 and waiting while preparing a written adoption plan in S3.
The locked S3 text does not specify a consultation requirement; WAIT can also
reflect caution or preservation, so its procedural interpretation is not unique. It does not imply
that these choices are morally superior. Both actions must be supported; one
favorable result cannot substitute for the joint prediction.

25 new unselected backgrounds from the unchanged ten Beta marginals and Gaussian
copula R; draw seed 20261012. For every background, PD=.1 and .9 are applied to
both locked dilemmas. Configuration neutral, 25 backgrounds, one run per condition,
100 responses total. Schedule seed 20261013; reserved analysis seed 20261014
(the primary exact calculation uses no simulation). All ten descriptions and
the full original canonical harness remain; only numerical coordinates vary.
These are counterfactual interventions, not an untouched population sample.

Model gpt-5.4-mini-2026-03-17, temperature1, max600 output tokens, concurrency3,
SDK retries0, wrapper attempts1. Write-once responses and dispatches. Stop on
any terminal failure after the current batch; no retry, extension or outcome
selection. The source sweep and this confirmation are not pooled.

For each dilemma, primary effect is the mean paired high-minus-low difference
in the named target response. Report all25 background effects, endpoint rates,
positive/negative/tied pair counts and two-sided exact matched-binomial tests
(positive vs negative discordances under p=.5), Holm correction over two tests.
Simultaneous conservative 95% intervals: obtain two-sided Clopper-Pearson bounds
for positive and negative discordance probabilities, with .05/4 per marginal
over both dilemmas, then subtract opposite bounds. This covers both paired
effects jointly at least95% under independent identically sampled backgrounds.
Both effects require lower bounds above0 AND Holm p<.05 for joint confirmation.
All ties yield p=1 and a nondegenerate interval. No gradient or paraphrase
equivalence claim is tested by this endpoint-only design.

The exact method was selected before these data, following the small discrete
bootstrap concern in diagnostics. It does not overwrite either prior analysis.
Generalization remains to this harness, model and source distribution. Existing
dilemmas are reused: this tests fresh-profile replication, not unseen-task
transfer. S2 and S3 option meanings differ, but this alone does not rule out all
lexical/instruction-following explanations or prove internal ethical understanding.

## Five synthetic review perspectives

Decision: independently test the newly observed process-oriented PD effects in
both dilemmas without changing the original validity claim or selecting profiles.

- Linden: PD's process/outcome meaning supports this interpretation; procedural
  preference must not be equated with moral goodness or intrinsic understanding.
- Osei: new backgrounds permit replication; the reused dilemmas limit transfer.
  Preserve their exploratory origin and report either failure without extension.
- Tanaka: paired discrete inference with a two-test family avoids relying on
  the observed small-sample bootstrap tail behavior; conservative bounds reduce power.
- Renna: different context-appropriate actions make this informative about the
  structural objective, though it cannot uniquely identify internal mechanisms.
- Okafor: unchanged engine and prompts isolate numerical intervention;100 calls
  are feasible inside the cap with explicit inventories and archived failures.

Disagreement: a replicated PD contrast could justify a local encoding claim,
but would not validate the entire architecture. Resolution: report it as local
behavioral evidence consistent with process-oriented normative parameterization;
retain all-ten negatives, diagnostic weaknesses and the unresolved original gate.
These are synthetic review roles, not external experts or human sign-off.
