# Confirmation after the exploratory pilot

This is a reviewable proposal after observing the pilot, not a frozen or queued
confirmatory run. The pilot's source and responses remain unchanged.

The narrow claim ready for independent confirmation is: **the complete profile
injection package changes decision distributions under specified environmental
contexts on these dilemmas**. It is not yet a confirmatory test of intrinsic
ethical understanding, human resemblance, or superiority to a postfilter.

## Lowest-cost defensible next step

Retain both anchors and all three simple tasks, with E/U conditions. Use **400
fresh paired profiles**, independent root seed and independent calls: 400 × 3 ×
2 × 2 = **4,800 calls**. Keep the pilot out of the confirmatory estimator.
At the pilot's average observed cost, conservative accounting projects roughly
**$4.74**; reserve up to **$10 operationally**, within the aggregate $100 cap.
This extrapolation is not a maximum-token guarantee or provider invoice.

Freeze six E−U comparisons as a single primary family; two-sided exact paired
binary tests with Holm family-wise correction at 0.05. Report risk differences
and intervals, failure rates and all S1 results even if saturated. Separately
specify environment-by-encoding contrasts before collection; do not relabel an
exploratory interaction as an original primary hypothesis. Define the intended
missing-response estimator and validate coverage near ceiling probabilities.

A provisional target of a 10-percentage-point difference is a design judgment,
not a result-derived fact or universally meaningful ethical effect. Power depends
on how often matched responses disagree, not just their mean difference.
An exact calculation at the conservative per-comparison level 0.05/6 gives:

| Fresh profiles | True difference | Pair disagreement | Power |
|---|---|---|---|
| 200 | 10 points | 30% | 44% |
| 400 | 10 points | 30% | 83% |
| 200 | 15 points | 30% | 89% |
| 200 | 20 points | 40% | 97% |

These are planning scenarios, not guarantees. The unusually low disagreement in
some pilot controls must not be assumed to persist. The 400-profile proposal
costs little more while avoiding sizing only for the largest pilot effect.
The [exact calculation](../../code/phase2_confirmation_power.py) reads no pilot
responses; [all scenarios](analysis/confirmation_power_scenarios.json) are retained.

## Group study and wider thesis

The immediate group work is offline: separate vote and evidence field validity;
make proposed versus adopted amendments unambiguous; retain model contradictions
as findings; test against the preserved malformed responses and state examples.
A changed prompt or output schema requires a new frozen designation and disclosed
harness change. Never “repair” an ethical choice by substituting the desired one.

Twenty groups per cell would cost roughly $14.24 at the pilot's group average,
before any larger prompt or output cost. This is still a modest group sample,
not automatically a conclusive test. For a broad group-effect claim, choose a
primary endpoint and calculate group-level power; additional rounds or agents
inside a group do not substitute for independent groups. C1 consensus/time and
state-consistency failures may be more informative than saturated final choices,
but selecting those now is exploratory and must be declared prospectively next.

The earlier ten-environment proposal remains available for wider coverage. It
requires its separate configuration-description review and costs more; two
anchors cannot replace the original five-axis survey without disclosing that
scope difference. Neither this small confirmation nor the earlier $27 package
contains validated human comparisons or reviewed moral coding. Those remain
Phases 3–4, with their own evidence and human sign-off.

Before launch: settle the scoped claim and effect-size target; complete the
estimator and repaired state tests; freeze and preregister the confirmatory plan;
carry forward the pilot's **$4.15084065** in the aggregate budget ledger. The
remaining budget is a ceiling, not a spending target.
