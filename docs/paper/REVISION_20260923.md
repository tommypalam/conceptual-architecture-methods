## Argument and evidence

The title is now **Which Part of a Prompt Carries Behaviour?**, with the subtitle
**Separating Names, Numbers, and Definitions in Structured Prompts**. The
paper leads on controlled input attribution. It distinguishes endpoint-definition
sensitivity from semantic understanding and from a decomposition of the whole
profile effect.

Corrections are grounded in the existing records:

- `experiments/phase7_understanding/semantics_r1/PROTOCOL.md` explicitly
  prespecified CANON-minus-variant direct contrasts. The previous paper's
  implication that the key comparison was introduced only later was wrong.
  The item-level robustness analyses remain labelled post-hoc.
- `floor_lift_r1/ASSESSMENT.md` and its per-cell records show mixed sensitivity
  to the twin clauses. PD is more deterred than SQ on `rest_break` and less
  deterred on `tool_library`; the low arms also contribute to the difference
  in endpoint effects. The paper no longer calls PD uniformly less sensitive
  or a demonstrated status-quo construct. The locked rule returned `mixed`.
- `probe_fields_r1/ASSESSMENT.md` explicitly describes SQ-versus-PD magnitude
  comparisons as descriptive across different populations and slots. The
  paper no longer claims an invented field outperforms PD in a controlled test.
- TfA/MoR follow-ups used both more profiles and a smaller Holm family than the
  original eight-field sweep. The revised text states both changes and avoids
  asserting known false-positive/false-negative counts from significance alone.
- Equal aggregate payoffs imply no aggregate-payoff preference, not indifference
  under minimax or distributional rules. A deterministic unprofiled baseline can
  move in one direction. Position pairing does not eliminate interactions.
- The account of the examples experiment is limited to in-context conditioning.
  A graded example-composition effect would not by itself establish a persistent
  disposition. Both order aggregation and the original analysis unit are disclosed.

The central estimates are unchanged. All nine base stimuli, seven twin scenarios,
the actual central-experiment system prompt, and four complete probe definitions
are now in the appendices. Stimuli were extracted from frozen request payloads;
the shared framing and unchanged twin option texts were checked programmatically.
