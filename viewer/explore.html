How the agents chose

60 matched agents per arm on this task. The bar shows the chosen metric, not a universal ethics score.

Every available action sacrifices at least one fixed standard. A net score balances positive and negative categories; strict scoring rejects any negative trigger. These are stipulated conventions.

Evidence, including the limits

What changed. What didn’t.

The thesis separates behavioural influence, moral trade-offs and the stronger claims still requiring evidence.

Observed

Profiles can change decisions.

The Phase 2 confirmation collected 4,800 individual responses and 300 group simulations. Six primary individual contrasts passed correction; one of seven secondary group contrasts did.

Phase 2 assessment ↗
Task-dependent

Honesty can come at a cost.

On the witness task, numeric profiles increased the net-score good rate from 23.3% to 75%. Across the two tasks, profiled agents deceived less and accepted more harm.

The first safety study showed no clear E−U effect. This does not establish that profiled agents are morally better.

Unresolved

Numbers have no proven special advantage.

Equivalent prose also shifts choices. The representation diagnostic established neither a clear numeric-versus-prose effect nor equivalence.

Representation assessment ↗
A measured boundary

Some baselines never budge.

Six fresh Phase 3 dilemmas each produced 25 identical unprofiled answers. Phase 3 closed for a limited measurement objective; the original benchmark battery did not pass.

A deterministic baseline can still differ from an encoded arm. It limits some planned comparisons; it does not make every effect impossible.

Scoped Phase 3 closure ↗
What this research does not establish

Intrinsic ethical understanding, human behavioural equivalence, universal moral truth, or a generally superior numerical architecture. The latest profiled moral experiment covers six screened situations, 40 agents and one model; the interactive examples come from earlier studies. Group moral evaluation is separate from the Phase 2 replays.
