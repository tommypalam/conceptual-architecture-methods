## Claim being tested

Under fixed canonical definitions, model, neutral context and locked dilemmas,
do numerical parameter interventions produce their theory-predicted behavioural
effects across previously untested complete profile backgrounds? Do intermediate
values also influence behaviour, beyond an extreme-endpoint switch?

Success would support **conditional behavioural encoding** for the contrasts
actually supported: an explicitly defined parameter representation systematically
controls decisions under the tested interface. It would not identify internal
mental understanding, establish moral competence, or prove that every possible
prompt-conditioning explanation is false. This is a fresh confirmation study
after development, not an independently conceived or externally preregistered
study. The full prior evidence, including unsuccessful candidates, remains visible.

## Frozen analysis and decision rules

The uncertainty unit is the **background**, not the individual call. Each background
contributes equally after averaging its two block observations. All 40 backgrounds
and all nine predictions appear in every final report, including contrary effects.

Estimate two contrasts for each prediction, oriented toward its target label:

1. Primary endpoint effect: rate(.9) minus rate(.1).
2. Prespecified intermediate-range effect: rate(.7) minus rate(.3).

Use 100,000 shared bootstrap resamples of whole backgrounds, analysis seed
**20261004**, NumPy linear quantiles. Report nominal 95% intervals and **99.7222%
individual intervals** (Bonferroni over all 18 contrasts, family alpha .05).
This conservative family prevents selecting a favourable endpoint/interior result
afterward. Percentile-bootstrap coverage is approximate at 40 backgrounds; it is
not an exact finite-sample guarantee. Retain all five rates, all background effects,
both block effects, and actual collection timestamps. Do not fit a straight logit
slope as though it necessarily describes a potentially threshold-like curve.

Endpoint confirmation for a prediction requires its adjusted lower bound >0 and
positive endpoint effects in both complete blocks. This is the same directional
logic as the development transfer study, now protected across the larger family.
Report magnitudes and intervals, not just a count of supported predictions.

Evidence extending through intermediate values requires endpoint confirmation,
an adjusted intermediate-effect lower bound >0, and nondecreasing observed rates
across all five levels. This is a conservative description of the sampled curve,
not a proof of global monotonicity. If the ordering condition misses, report the
observed shape prominently: it may reflect sampling noise, a true reversal or an
endpoint threshold, and does not demonstrate the absence of parameter influence.
Do not relax this condition or collect extras once outcomes are known.

Opposite-direction specificity in S3 is supported only if BOTH MoR->ADOPT and
RE->WAIT independently meet the endpoint rule. MoR alone plus a null RE does not
establish opposing controllability. Similarly, opposite S2 targets are interpreted
only when their individual predictions are supported. These conjunctions add no
new unadjusted tests and do not prove an internal semantic mechanism.

There is no prechosen quota of favourable predictions to label the architecture
valid. State precisely which parameter/problem effects confirm, which extend
through intermediate levels, and which remain unresolved or contrary. The test
addresses numerical conditioning under canonical wording. It does not rerun
paraphrase equivalence, representation retention or the reasoning audit, so it
cannot by itself turn the original four-part Phase 1.5 gate into a pass.
