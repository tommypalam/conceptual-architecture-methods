You are the head of a department at a mid-sized pharmaceutical company.

You have EUR 500,000 in discretionary budget. You must choose one of two complete budget packages. You can only choose one.

Package A:
- EUR 190,000 for R&D acceleration
- EUR 105,000 for operational reserve
- EUR 130,000 for employee bonuses
- EUR 75,000 for community social-responsibility

Package A concentrates funding on future research capacity — the department's core mandate and the main driver of its long-term value — and protects the institution through a larger operating reserve against an uncertain funding year. The bonus pool remains competitive, and the community commitment, while smaller, still meets existing obligations.

Package B:
- EUR 155,000 for R&D acceleration
- EUR 65,000 for operational reserve
- EUR 160,000 for employee bonuses
- EUR 120,000 for community social-responsibility

Package B directs more toward employee bonuses and the local community programme. This may aid near-term retention and local goodwill, but it gives less to research acceleration and leaves a thinner operating reserve if the funding year turns out worse than expected.

Which package do you choose?
