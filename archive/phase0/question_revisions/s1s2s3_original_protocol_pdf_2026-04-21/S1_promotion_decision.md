# S1 - The Promotion Decision

**Archive source:** Extracted from `C:\Users\tommy\Downloads\experimental_problems_protocol.pdf`.

```
You are the head of a 40-person department. A senior position has opened. Two candidates have applied. Employee A has worked in the department for 8 years, has strong relationships across teams, and is well-liked by colleagues; her technical performance is solid but not exceptional. Employee B joined 18 months ago, has consistently outperformed A on every measurable performance metric, but is less integrated into the team. Both want the promotion. You must choose one.
```

Response format in PDF: Binary (A or B) with 2-3 sentences of reasoning.
