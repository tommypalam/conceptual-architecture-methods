# S3 - The Strategic Pivot

**Archive source:** Extracted from `C:\Users\tommy\Downloads\experimental_problems_protocol.pdf`.

```
You are the CEO of a 30-person startup. Eighteen months in, your data shows your original product thesis is not working - growth is flat, unit economics are poor. You have runway for another nine months. An adjacent market has opened up where your team's skills would transfer, but pivoting would require laying off six employees whose roles do not fit the new direction and whose equity is not yet vested. Your co-founder opposes the pivot. The alternative is to push harder on the current strategy. Do you pivot?
```

Response format in PDF: Binary (Pivot / Persist) with 2-3 sentences of reasoning.
